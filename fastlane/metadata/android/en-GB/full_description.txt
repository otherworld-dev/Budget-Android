Photograph a paper receipt and save it straight to Budget on your own Nextcloud,
with the photo attached to the transaction.

Budget Companion works with the Budget app for Nextcloud. It does one job:
getting a transaction into Budget from your phone — usually by photographing the
receipt, with a quick manual form for the ones that have no receipt at all.
Everything else — budgets, reports, bills, charts — stays in Budget's web
interface.

• Snap a receipt, or share an image to the app from anywhere
• Your Budget server reads the receipt and fills in merchant, date and total
• Check it, adjust anything, and save
• Add a transaction by hand in seconds when there's no receipt to photograph
• Works offline: photos and entries are queued and sent when you're back online
• See recent transactions to confirm what was saved

Receipt reading happens on your own Nextcloud server, using whatever backend
your Budget installation is configured with. This app never sends your receipts
anywhere else, holds no API keys, and collects no data about you.

You'll need a Nextcloud server with the Budget app installed.
